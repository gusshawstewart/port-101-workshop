# Domain

A generic software catalog: a handful of services (`checkout-service`,
`auth-service`, `notifications-service`, `billing-service`,
`search-service`, `inventory-service`) with realistic, uneven metadata —
some are well-documented and owned, some are missing a README or an
on-call owner, some are still Experimental.

That unevenness is intentional: it's what makes a maturity scorecard show a
real spread of levels instead of everything landing on Gold, and it gives
the AI workflow something concrete to flag.

There's no fictional company or story here on purpose — this is meant to be
a recognizable, minimal starting point for anyone about to run their own
Port POC with their own real services.
